Map Skills Concepts

Map skills help us read, understand, and use maps correctly. They help us identify places, direction, distance, symbols, landforms, and human activities shown on a map.

Direction

Direction helps us know where places are in relation to one another. Maps often use north, south, east, and west.

Scale

Scale shows the relationship between distance on the map and real distance on the ground.

Relief

Relief shows the shape and height of the land, such as mountains, valleys, and hills.

Natural and human features

Natural features include rivers, forests, and mountains. Human features include roads, farms, and settlements.

What are map skills?

Map skills are the skills we use to read, understand, and interpret maps correctly. They help us know where places are, how far apart they are, and what different symbols and features on the map mean.

Good map skills include understanding direction, scale, symbols, relief, and the difference between natural and human features. These skills are important in Geography because they help us use maps to study real places and landscapes.

Direction

Helps us know location using north, south, east, and west.

Distance

Scale helps us work out how far places are from one another.

Features

Maps show both natural features and human features.

Relief

Relief helps us understand the shape and height of the land.
