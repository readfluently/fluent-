Photosynthesis Concepts

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food. It mainly takes place in the leaves and needs sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide.

Sunlight

Sunlight provides the energy that drives photosynthesis.

Carbon dioxide

Carbon dioxide is a gas from the air that enters the leaves.

Water

Water is absorbed by the roots and moved through the plant.

Glucose and oxygen

The plant makes food called glucose and releases oxygen into the air.

What is photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food. Plants use sunlight as energy, take in carbon dioxide from the air, and absorb water through their roots.

During this process, plants make a kind of food called glucose. At the same time, they release oxygen into the air. This process is very important because it helps plants grow and also provides oxygen for living things.

Where it happens

Photosynthesis mainly happens in the green leaves of a plant.

What is needed

Sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide are needed for photosynthesis.

What is made

The plant makes glucose, which is its food.

What is released

The plant releases oxygen into the air.
