What is a topographic map?

A topographic map is a map that shows natural and human-made features, and it also shows the height and shape of the land.

Contour lines

Contour lines join places of equal height above sea level. They help us see whether land is steep, gentle, low, or high.

Natural features

These include hills, rivers, valleys, mountains, forests, and lakes.

Human-made features

These include roads, bridges, railways, dams, settlements, and buildings.

Scale and direction

A topographic map helps us measure distance and locate places using direction.
